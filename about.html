Our Mission

Bridging AI & Student Success

SkillOS is an experimental student environment matching standard course curriculum tracks with context-aware LLM coaches.

The Idea

Most online education catalogs fail due to lack of personalization and immediate help. By integrating a 24/7 AI tutor powered by Gemini directly inside the curriculum view, students get their bugs fixed, logic puzzles explained, and diagnostic tests generated in real-time.

Our Stack

SkillOS runs on clean modern HTML5 components, CSS design tokens, modular ES Javascript, PostgreSQL Database, Express REST APIs, and Google Gemini AI API endpoints.
